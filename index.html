Privacy Policy

Puffy's Sesh & Planner · Last updated November 11, 2026

Puffy's Sesh & Planner ("the app") is made by Knicknak Enterprises LLC, an Oklahoma limited liability company doing business as Puffy's ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app and our website do with your information. The short version: your journal stays on your device, you don't need an account, and we don't use ad trackers or sell your information.

Information that stays on your device

Everything you put in your journal is stored on your phone or in your browser, not on our servers. That includes your sessions, ratings, notes, effects, photos, spending, goals, try list, saved shops, custom fields, settings and your name in the app. If you set a PIN, only a one-way code made from it is kept on your device; the PIN itself is never stored or sent. Your birthday is checked on your device when you first open the app and is never stored or sent. We can't see your journal and we can't recover it for you.

Information that leaves your device

  • Strain database. When you search, browse or import strains, the app sends your search words and filters to our server to get results.
  • Your IP address. Every request includes it. We use it briefly to limit abuse, such as too many requests a minute, and don't store it with anything you do in the app.
  • Shop map. If you search a city, state or ZIP, we receive what you typed. If you tap "Use my location", your phone asks your permission first, and the app rounds your location to about 1 km (roughly a neighborhood) before sending it. Our server uses it only to find nearby shops and keeps the results for a few hours under the rounded area so repeat searches are faster. Map pictures load from OpenFreeMap or OpenStreetMap, which see your IP address and the part of the map you're viewing.
  • Your approximate state. Our host, Cloudflare, tells our server which US state a request comes from. We use it only to choose which sponsored cards may show in your state, and we don't store it.
  • Sponsored card counts. When a sponsored card is on your screen or you tap it, the app sends that card's id and a count. We keep only daily totals per card, with no device id, name or IP address. Sponsors never receive information about you from us.
  • Problem reports. If you report a problem with a shop, we receive the shop, what you picked and anything you write. Reports never include your location. Please don't include personal details.
  • Support messages. If you contact us through the support form, we receive your message, the topic, the app version and your type of device, plus your email address only if you give one so we can reply. We use them only to help you and delete every message automatically 90 days after it arrives.
  • Purchases. Upgrades are handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies. We never receive your card or store account details; the app only learns which plan is active on your device.
  • Other companies. Cloudflare hosts our website and server and may keep request logs for a limited time. Google Fonts provides the fonts, so your browser asks Google for them. Links you open, such as a sponsor's site, a shop's website or your maps app, have their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • We don't require an account.
  • We don't use advertising or analytics trackers, and we don't build profiles about you.
  • We don't sell or rent your information. The app shows clearly labeled sponsored cards from local businesses, chosen only by state.

Backups

"Back up everything" saves your journal to a file on your device. You choose where it goes and who you share it with. We never receive your backup.

Age

The app is for adults 21 and older, where adult use is legal. It is not meant for anyone under 21, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your information

Because your journal lives on your device, deleting the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) deletes it; Me → About → Erase all my data does the same inside the app. Support messages are deleted after 90 days. To ask us to delete a report or message sooner, or about any privacy rights your state gives you, contact us through our Support page.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for big changes, tell you in the app.

Common questions

Where is my journal saved?

On your device only. There's no account and no cloud copy, so make backups from Me → Your data → Back up everything.

How do I move to a new phone?

On your old phone, tap Back up everything and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. On the new phone, open the app and tap Restore from backup.

I bought an upgrade. How do I get it back?

Upgrades are tied to your App Store or Google Play account. Sign in with the same store account on your new device to restore it.

A shop on the map is wrong or closed.

Open the shop on the Map tab and tap Report. Tell us what's wrong and we'll fix it.

How do I get rid of the example sessions?

Go to Me → Your data → Clear example sessions.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app from your phone, or clear the site's data in your browser if you use the web version. That removes everything.
